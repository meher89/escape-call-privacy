Escape Call – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Privacy summary: Escape Call is a call simulation application. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information with us.

1. Introduction

Escape Call ("the App") is a mobile application that simulates incoming phone calls for entertainment and practical purposes. The App allows users to create and schedule simulated calls, customize caller information, and display a simulated incoming call screen.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Escape Call handles information when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

Escape Call does not collect or transmit personal information to us or to our servers.

The App does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal information to use its features.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Information that you enter into the App, such as a simulated caller name, phone number, photo, ringtone preference, or scheduled call information, may be stored locally on your device so that the App can provide its features.

This information is not transmitted to us and is not accessible to us.

4. Contacts and Photos

If you choose to use features that allow you to select a contact or photo from your device, the App uses the relevant Android system functionality to provide that feature.

Any information selected by you is used locally by the App and is not uploaded to or transmitted to our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Escape Call does not use third-party services to collect or transmit personal information on our behalf.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

7. Data Security

Because Escape Call does not transmit personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a remote database containing users' personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Escape Call does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

9. Data Deletion

Since Escape Call does not transmit personal information to our servers, we do not maintain user accounts or personal information that requires deletion from our systems.

Locally stored App data can be removed by uninstalling the App or by clearing the App's data through Android system settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page, together with an updated "Last updated" date.
